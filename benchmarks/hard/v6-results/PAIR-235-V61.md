## Verdict: mechanism CONFIRMED on its cell; arm delta NOT above the noise floor — 0.0.2.35 is NOT adopted

Mean 61.3 -> 63.3 (**+2.0**). The measured resolution of this exact comparison is ~2 cells (same stamp,
same three seeds, twice: 12 of 231 cells flip, mean moves 1.4). +2.0 sits AT that floor, not above it,
and the pooled sign test (9W/3L, p=0.146) resolves nothing at arm level.

The pre-registered hypothesis (written before any `h*` row — see `PAIR-234-V61.md`) was deliberately
narrower than a headline: *exactly one cell — B-ex6-06 — flips in all three candidate seeds, carrying
`contour:v235_b_table_total`; expected mean +1.0.* The **cell-level claim passed exactly**: `B-ex6-06`
was 0/3 strict in control (note `fields_mismatch:available_total` in all three seeds — this pair adds
three more failing seeds to its 6-seed chronic record) and 3/3 strict in the candidate, with the v235
event on every converted row and zero extra calls. That is the strongest mechanism evidence produced by
the series so far: deterministic, prompt-grounded, event-attributed.

But the pair also refuted the *clean-pattern* condition the pre-registration set: **four off-target cells
flipped with no 2.35 event on them** (A-ex6-00 +1, A-ex6-08 +2, F-ex6-00 +1, J-ex6-06 -1), so the observed
+2.0 decomposes as +1.0 event-attributed plus +1.0 pure seed resampling. Under the pre-registered rule
("a flip without the event, or an off-target cell moving, refutes and freezes") and the standing
noise-floor rule (do not adopt without clearing ~2 cells), the stamp does not enter the adopted line.

Adoption state: `B_TABLE_AGGREGATE` frozen to stamp 2.35 alone (`CONTOUR_REVISION == (2, 35)`,
`UNADOPTED_FEATURES["b_table_aggregate"]`), contract-tested in `gate_v49_feature_adoption_test.py`;
the adopted line stays `0.0.2.29`. This is a LINE decision, not a mechanism verdict: the repair remains
reproducible and named at stamp `0.0.2.35`, and re-adoption would be a one-flag owner call, not a rebuild.
