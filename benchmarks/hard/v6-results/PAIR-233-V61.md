## Verdict: +1.3 cells is under the noise floor AND the mechanism never fired — 0.0.2.33 is NOT adopted

Mean 61.7 → 63.0. The measured resolution of this exact comparison is ~2 cells (same stamp, same three
seeds, twice: 12 of 231 cells flip, mean moves 1.4). +1.3 sits below that floor; per-seed sign tests
(3W/0L p=0.25, 1W/0L p=1.0, 1W/1L p=1.0; pooled 5W/1L p=0.22) resolve nothing. Stated plainly: this
pair did not show that 0.0.2.33 helps, and it is not adopted as an improvement.

Stronger statement than the mean alone allows: **the pair could not have measured the mechanism at
all.** The ledger `events` column carries zero `v233_a_tariff_table` hits in all three candidate seeds.
Every flipped cell (A-ex6-00, A-ex6-08, H-ex6-01, H-ex6-04) therefore carries no 2.33 event — those
flips are seed/endpoint noise, not the change. Flip count 10 → 7 is directional for the same reason.

## Why it never fired: counterfactual replay on the saved answer bytes

Replaying `a_arith_by_code` (stamp 2.33, workspace bank `f4553aab304f60d7`) over the stored A-ex6-00
answers — the only bank prompt whose grammar the 2.33 parser accepts (coverage 1 of 9 A tasks; the
other 8 refuse at the grammar step, which is the designed fail-closed behaviour):

| arm | as-run score | membership guard | replay result |
|---|---|---|---|
| control seed 0 | pass | keys not exact (extra/other keys) | refuses, touches nothing |
| control seed 1 | pass | keys exact | already equal → no-op, no event |
| control seed 2 | **fail** `fields_mismatch:surcharges,total` | keys exact | **would repair** — values+total recomputed from the prompt's own table, event `v233_a_tariff_table` |
| candidate seed 0 | pass | keys not exact | refuses, touches nothing |
| candidate seed 1 | pass | keys exact | already equal → no-op |
| candidate seed 2 | pass | keys exact | already equal → no-op |

So the mechanism is correctly implemented on saved bytes — on the failed control seed it would have
converted exactly its target class, and it no-ops on already-correct answers — but in the candidate
arm the model happened to answer that cell correctly on all three seeds, so the repair never had
anything to fix. The arm delta therefore contains zero on-target cells. The counterfactual row is a
replay, not a measurement; it is not quotable as an effect.

What this pair also shows about power: a candidate whose mechanism never fires is indistinguishable
from its control modulo seed noise — +1.3 for a no-op arm is a direct empirical lower bound on how
much pure resampling moves this scoreboard, consistent with the ~2-cell floor.
