## Verdict: +1.3 cells is under the noise floor — 0.0.2.32 is NOT adopted

Mean 61.0 → 62.3. The measured resolution of this exact comparison is ~2 cells (same stamp, same three
seeds, twice: 12 of 231 cells flip, mean moves 1.4). +1.3 sits below that floor; per-seed sign tests
(3W/1L p=0.63, 3W/0L p=0.25, 0W/1L p=1.0; pooled 6W/2L p=0.29) resolve nothing. Stated plainly: this
pair did not show that 0.0.2.32 helps, and it is not adopted as an improvement.

What the pair does establish, cell by cell:

- The mechanism fired exactly once in three seeds (`contour:v20_v232_j_rerank_by_prompt`, seed 0) and it
  fixed exactly its class: `J-ex6-06` seed 0 — the model's own three ids `["t1","t3","t4"]`-class list in
  the wrong order, re-sorted from the prompt's own table, passed 2/3 → 3/3. In the control arm the same
  cell lost because the format-repair chain ended in `[]`.
- The membership guard held: `set(ranked) == set(ids)` is required at the call site, the grammar refused
  everywhere else (zero `v232_j_membership_untouched` side effects on other rows), and bank coverage
  stays 3/7 reproduces, 4/7 refuses, **0 mis-sorts** (`gate_v48`, `gate_v49`).
- The other five flipped cells (A-ex6-00, C-ex6-00, H-ex6-03, H-ex6-04, K-ex6-03) carry no 2.32 event —
  they are seed/endpoint noise, not the change. Flip count 11 → 7 is directional only for the same reason.
