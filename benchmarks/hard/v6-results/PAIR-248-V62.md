## Mechanism (one substantive change)

`B_ECHO_RESTORE = STAMP_REVISION >= (2, 51)` re-arms ONLY the echo grammar of the 2.34 B field
repair. When the prompt states `добавь поле "X": сумма длин Y по всем Z` (plus at most one
`удали "W"`) and embeds a parseable JSON literal, the answer is rebuilt **from the prompt's own
object**: `Z` verbatim from the literal, `X` recomputed as the sum of `len(Y)` over the literal's
items, `W` dropped. It refuses — text untouched, no event — on grammar absence, more than one
drop, an unparseable literal, non-list items, an item missing the length field, a non-dict answer,
any top-level key the literal+declared-edit never names, or an already-equal answer (zero fires
on passes). Zero model tokens; the reference is never read; the reachable surface is closed by
construction: the coverage census in `gate_v65_b_echo_restore_test.py` pins the grammar to match
EXACTLY one of the 77 bank prompts (B-ex6-05). The config-fields grammar stays frozen to exactly
(2,34). Event: `contour:v51_b_echo_restore` — the `v234_b_echo_count` name belongs to the 2.34
arm bytes and was not rewritten.
