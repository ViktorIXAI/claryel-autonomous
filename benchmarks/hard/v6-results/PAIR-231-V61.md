## Verdict: 0.0.2.31 is a second negative result, and it says something specific

The mechanism fired (that part is not in doubt): `v231_selfconsist_*` events on every C/J row — 32
unanimous, 4 plurality-equals-original, 1 no-majority, and **8 votes taken** (7 category C, 1 category J),
replacing the answer in 5 cells.

What the votes did: it replaced correct answers with wrong ones. `C-ex6-01` 2/3 → 0/3, `C-ex6-00` 2/3 → 1/3,
`C-ex6-03` 3/3 → 2/3, `J-ex6-04` 3/3 → 2/3. Mean 61.7 → 59.0; per-seed sign tests 1W/4L, 2W/5L.

The reason is worth recording: on these tasks the model is not noisy, it is **biased**. Its samples agree on
the wrong option set more often than they agree on the right one, so a plurality vote amplifies its own
mistake. Self-consistency is the right tool for variance and the wrong tool for bias, and this bank
separates the two cleanly: the same class of cells that flips for one model agrees wrong for another.

## Noise floor measured directly (this is the number the next experiment has to beat)

The control stamp 0.0.2.29 was now run twice over the same three seeds (the pair-230 control run and this
pair-231 control run). Identical stamp, identical seeds, different runs: **12 of 231 cells (5.2%) change
strict pass/fail**, and the per-seed totals move from 62/59/60 (mean 60.3) to 63/62/60 (mean 61.7).

Consequences, stated as limits:
- a contour-vs-contour pair on three seeds resolves differences of roughly ±1.5–2 cells and nothing finer.
  0.0.2.30 (−1.3) and 0.0.2.31 (−2.7) both sit at or beyond that floor, so neither is adopted, and the
  honest reading of 0.0.2.31 is "no benefit, probably a small harm, at 3 seeds".
- the headline arm margins do NOT live at this resolution: contour vs ChatGPT is +12.0 cells (CI
  +4.3..+19.8) and vs Grok +8.3 (CI +1.8..+15.0), four to six times the noise floor, from six contour
  seeds against three cloud seeds.
- to move the unresolved contour-vs-bare-basalt number (+5.9 cells, CI −0.5..+12.5), the next contour
  change has to be worth more than ~2 cells or be measured over more seeds. Smaller deltas are not
  measurable at three seeds and will be reported as unresolvable rather than as wins.

Next hypothesis, from the corrected cause table rather than from a guess: `J-ex6-*` order cells fail with
`order_partial` — right ids, wrong order — and the ordering rule is written in the task prompt (a table of
id/number pairs plus «по убыванию/по возрастанию»). A deterministic re-sort of the model's own id list
using only the prompt's own table is a zero-invention repair, unlike the vote. That becomes 0.0.2.32.
