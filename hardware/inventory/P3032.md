## GPU fit rule (maintainer decision, 2026-11-03)

HP Z220 chassis fan headers cannot be speed-controlled from Linux (see
`results/p3032-p100-bringup-thermal.md`). Cards without their own managed
cooling (Tesla P100 class) must not be deployed here. Self-cooling cards
(RTX 3060 12 GB verified: effective + near-silent, dense quiet case) are
the right pairing for this board.
