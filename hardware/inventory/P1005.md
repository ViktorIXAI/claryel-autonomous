**First-run benchmark (2026-11-01, cold engine, empty KV, tuned config below):**
with the original 65 536-context config: prefill 234 tok/s and decode 21.3
tok/s at a 32 768-token prompt (wall 143 s, draft acceptance 38/48); warm
ladder decode 22.2–22.5 tok/s at 8 k–32 k, prefill plateau ≈ 220 tok/s.
After porting the 12 GB-card recipe (same levers as the RTX 3060 box:
`--resident-budget-gib 20` + `STRATA_UNBUFFERED_LOAD=1` + `--prefill 4096` +
`--pool-workers 16` + `--max-context 32768`): cold ladder prefill
209 / 269 / 285 tok/s and decode 15.6 / 21.6 / 26.5 tok/s at 2 k / 8 k / 16 k
(warm decode peak 27). Prefill +30–35%, decode +10–20%.

**Speed records (2026-11-02, engine log lines, raw model, no harness):**
decode peak 47.2 tok/s; structured/agent-style answers sustain 40.7–43.8
tok/s (draft acceptance 96–100%); free-form prose 33.9–37.2 tok/s; prefill
294.0 tok/s at a 16 448-token cold prompt. Full table, per-row configs and
log excerpts: results/p1005-speed-records.md. Production config now runs
`--resident-budget-gib 25` + `--memory-limit-mib 28672` +
`--vram-reserve-mib 512` + `STRATA_ROUTE_PRIOR=1.5` (residency-biased
routing doubles prose/worst-case decode; carries the upstream #4402
repetition/agent-quality cost — quality series run without it). 60–70 tok/s
decode is not reachable on sm_60: IQ3_S decode is dequant-bound and Pascal
has no bf16/tensor path, and the 3060's 55–72 figure additionally relied on
the gpu-unlock module (GA102–GA106 only). GPU 45 °C, 33.6 W, VRAM 15.5 /
16 GiB with everything resident — no VRAM cap below the full card.

**Tuning levers that matter on sm_60 + 31 GiB RAM** (measured 2026-10-20,
re-confirmed 2026-11-01): `--resident-experts` (page-locks hottest experts,
biggest decode lever) with an explicit `--resident-budget-gib` (without it
`STRATA_UNBUFFERED_LOAD=1` is silently ignored by the engine),
`--vram-reserve-mib 960` + `--max-context 32768`
(the 262 144 KV reservation starves the expert cache), `STRATA_Q8_SM60=0`
(packed Q8 copies buy nothing on PCIe Pascal), `STRATA_DENSE_F32=1`.
Engine 0.1.55 raised decode from ~15 to ~22 tok/s at 16 k–32 k versus the
0.1.46/0.1.47 baseline on the same box.

**Full contour v2.1 series (2026-11-02, 2421 pairs, same plan/seeds as the
B2003 run):** mean content 0.9414, perfect 81.7%, format_ok 99.8%,
1.41 calls/task, 1127 tokens/task, wall 21.1 s/task. Paired vs B2003:
46 better / 118 worse / 2257 ties, mean delta −0.0067 — contour quality
is hardware-portable; the Pascal card pays in wall-clock (2.7×), not
correctness. Full report: results/p1005-v21-full.md.

after excluding the two known G-ve-13/14 capacity rejects. F returned **1.00** on

is in progress under the unlocked 187 W ceiling; the first run's 1 Hz sampler
