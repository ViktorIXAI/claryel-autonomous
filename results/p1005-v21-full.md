# P1005 full contour v2.1 series — cross-box validation vs B2003

Full PARB main-series run on P1005 (Tesla P100 16 GB / Xeon E5-2690 v4),
contour v2.1, identical plan to the completed B2003 run: 2421 (task, rep)
pairs, 178 bank tasks, 11 categories, same seeds. Run 2026-11-02,
08:23–23:55 UTC (two segments: 12 h limit hit at 1993 pairs, resumed from
state; total engine time 53 460 s). One client at a time, endpoint idle
during the run.

Serve config for the quality series (deliberately not the speed config):
engine 0.1.55 cuda12, `--resident-budget-gib 20`, `--vram-reserve-mib 960`,
`--max-context 32768`, **no `STRATA_ROUTE_PRIOR`** (residency-biased
routing buys decode speed but costs agent quality — see
results/p1005-speed-records.md; quality series run without it on both
boxes).

Paired by (task, rep): **P1005 better 46, B2003 better 118, tie 2257**,
mean delta −0.0067. Verdict: contour quality is hardware-portable — the
same v2.1 on a 2016 datacenter card lands within 0.7 points of the RTX
3060 box on the same 2421 pairs; the cost is wall-clock (2.7× slower),
not correctness.
